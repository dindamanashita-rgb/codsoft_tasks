What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyberattack in which criminals pretend to be trusted people or organizations to steal sensitive information such as passwords, bank details and personal data.

Email Phishing

Fake emails that try to steal personal information.

Smishing

Fraudulent SMS messages containing suspicious links.

Vishing

Phone calls used to trick people into sharing secrets.

Spear Phishing

Targeted phishing messages designed for specific people.

How to Identify a Phishing Email

Example: Suspicious Email

From: support@unknown-example.com

Subject: URGENT! Your account will be blocked!

Hello User,

Your account will be suspended today. Click the link below immediately to verify your password.

This is a simulated example for awareness. Do not enter any real credentials.

Safety Tips

✓ Never share your password or OTP.

✓ Check the sender's email address carefully.

✓ Avoid clicking unknown links.

✓ Use multi-factor authentication.

✓ Keep your software updated.

✓ Verify suspicious requests through official channels.

Example Case Study

The Fake Bank Alert

A person receives a message claiming that their bank account will be blocked unless they verify their details. The message contains a link to a fake website.

Lesson: Never trust urgent messages asking for passwords, OTPs or banking information. Contact your bank through its official website or number.
